Plain-English Guide

Every term used on this dashboard, explained without jargon.

Open / High / Low / Close
The four prices of a trading day: where it started, its peak, its low, and where it ended.
Volume
How many shares were traded that day. Big volume = lots of buying and selling interest.
MA20 / MA50
The average price of the last 20 / 50 days. Smooths out daily noise so the underlying trend is easier to see.
RSI
A 0–100 momentum score. Above 70: possibly over-bought (due for a dip). Below 30: possibly over-sold (due for a bounce).
MACD
Compares short-term vs long-term momentum. When its lines cross, a trend change may be coming.
ARIMA
A classical statistics model that predicts the next price from recent prices and recent errors.
Prophet
A model that splits price into trend + repeating seasonal patterns, then extends each piece forward.
LSTM
A deep-learning network with built-in memory, designed to learn patterns in sequences — like prices.
XGBoost
A team of small decision trees that, combined, can learn complex non-linear patterns.
SVR
Support Vector Regression — fits a smooth curve through the data while tolerating small errors.
Test Window
The last 63 trading days, kept hidden from training — a fair final exam for every model.
MSE / RMSE
The average size of a model's prediction mistakes (lower = better). RMSE is in price units, e.g. "off by ₹24 per day".
Confidence Band
The shaded range where the price will probably stay. Wider = less certain.
Monte Carlo
Runs 50 random "what-if" futures to show the full range of possible outcomes, good and bad.
VaR 95%
Value at Risk: the loss that should only be exceeded 5% of the time.
Sharpe Ratio
Return earned per unit of risk taken. Above 1 is good; above 2 is excellent.
Buy & Hold
The do-nothing benchmark: buy once and wait. A strategy must beat this to be useful.
